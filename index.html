Firefox (WebAssembly)

The real Firefox browser engine, compiled to WebAssembly, running inside your browser — Chrome, Edge, Brave and Chromebooks.

The first start downloads Firefox and takes about 30–60 seconds — keep the tab open.
Press Enter to launch. Firefox draws its own tabs and address bar.
Firefox WebAssembly is an experimental demo by Puter (developer.puter.com), not an official Mozilla release.
